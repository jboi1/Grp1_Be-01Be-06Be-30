### Project Description:

This project is a web-based RPG centralized around practicing math skills, which will most likely be needed by anyone who wants to polish up on their math skills or wants to learn some new math. In the game, the player will fight against monsters, who have randomly generated math questions attached to their bodies, in waves by finding the answer to the question found on each monster. If the player doesn't kill monsters quickly enough, the monsters may reach the player and deal damage to the player until they die and the game ends. As the game progresses, the monsters have more health, deal more damage to the player, and show more difficult questions. However, the player also gets new weapons, which amplify their damage output such that the rarer it is, the bigger the damage the player deals per attack, every 5 waves, and items that may increase their maximum health, heal them, or increase their luck, every 2 waves. When the player reaches game over, like an arcade game, they will be asked to give a username, so they can be put on the leaderboard so the players can be ranked by how many waves they have completed.

### Goals:

Our project’s long term goal is to create a new way for people to learn mathematics while also having fun.  Through our project, users will learn to be more confident in their maths as well as useful skills from the game such as strategy and resource management.  Aside from this, our project aims to teach people math concepts that are not very well known as some of these can be used in the real world.  All in all, our project aims to teach people how to see mathematics in an interactive manner, apply mathematics in the real world, and sustain long term learning in math.

- Objective 1: Create a basic framework for the game which includes enemies, weapons, characters, and others.
- Objective 2: Incorporate accurate and useful math questions into the gameplay to help players learn math while playing.
- Objective 3: Expand the game’s progression by adding more gameplay.  Additionally, make the math concepts more and more complex as the game goes on.
- Objective 4 (Last priority): Enhance the game’s visuals without changing the core gameplay.
